SoCal Squash, where the sun meets the court.

SoCal Squash is the official US Squash affiliate for the game of squash in Southern California. We are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, dedicated to promoting squash and connecting the community. Our region spans as far north as San Luis Obispo, all the way down to San Diego.
